Lavender splash lab

Each frame is the app window at 4:3. Click one to see it full size (Esc or click to close). Everything is plain HTML, CSS and inline SVG, so the chosen splash can live in index.html and paint before the app's scripts load.

Exit into the app: scatter variants (option G)

The splash shows while the app starts, then plays its exit once the library is ready. Here the "ready" moment comes 2.4 s in so the idle loop is visible first; in the app it is whenever startup finishes, with a short minimum so a fast start does not flash. The splash is removed the moment its exit ends, so nothing is left over the app. Click a frame or Play to run it again.
